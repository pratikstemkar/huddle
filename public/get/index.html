How it works

From “we should go” to a locked plan, in four steps.

  1. Start a trip

    Give it a name like “Goa in March” and pick a colour. Know the dates already? Set them.

  2. Invite your people

    Share a short code or link on WhatsApp, or invite a whole group you’ve saved.

  3. Decide together

    Everyone taps the days they’re free and suggests places. Then you all vote.

  4. Lock it in

    The organiser locks the dates and the place. Now plan each day and split the bills.

Everything the trip needs

Each part of the trip has its own tab, and everyone sees changes the moment they happen.

Vote · Dates

See the days everyone can make

Each person taps the days they’re free. The calendar fills in darker where more of you overlap, and Huddle picks out the best stretch for you.

Vote · Places

Pick where to go, fairly

Anyone can suggest a place with a note, a link and a photo. Everyone votes, and the leader is plain to see. No more “I thought we said Gokarna.”

Trip

Lock it in and start counting down

When it’s decided, the organiser locks the dates and the place. Everyone gets the summary, a countdown and an Add to calendar button.

Plan

Fill in each day

Add plans to each day of the trip with a time and a note: sunrise at the beach, lunch booked for four. Days with nothing planned yet stand out.

Split

Split the costs, skip the maths

Log who paid for what and Huddle works out who owes whom, in as few payments as possible. Mark it settled when the money moves.

One shared album

Everyone adds their photos to the trip, sorted by day.

Groups

Save your college gang or flatmates once, then start trips with them in a tap.

Hangouts

Not a whole trip? Find a time for dinner or a match with your group.

Gentle nudges

Pip reminds the people who haven’t voted yet, so you don’t have to chase them.

Installing on Android
  1. Tap Download the app. The file is called huddle-…apk.
  2. Open it from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open Huddle and sign in. Updates arrive inside the app.
Using Huddle on iPhone
  1. The iPhone app isn’t on the App Store yet, so Huddle runs in Safari for now.
  2. Tap Open Huddle and sign in.
  3. To keep it on your home screen, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
